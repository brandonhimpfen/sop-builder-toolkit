## 1. Purpose

To provide a consistent process for reviewing and publishing routine
text updates to a public website.

## 2. Scope

Applies to routine edits to existing informational pages. It excludes
emergency notices, new applications, payment pages, legal notices, and
changes requiring development work.

## 3. Roles

  Role                  Responsibility
  --------------------- --------------------------------------------------
  Content requester     Supplies wording and context
  Web editor            Prepares, checks, and publishes approved updates
  Content owner         Confirms substantive accuracy
  Web Operations Lead   Handles exceptions and owns the process

## 4. Prerequisites

The requester identifies the page, requested change, reason, and content
owner. The web editor has authorized publishing access.

## 5. Procedure

1.  Record the request in the work-tracking system.
2.  Confirm the page and content owner.
3.  Review the request for completeness.
4.  For substantive changes, obtain content-owner confirmation.
5.  Prepare the update in the content-management system.
6.  Preview headings, links, formatting, and display.
7.  Publish the approved update.
8.  Open the public page and confirm the change.
9.  Record completion, publication date, and page link.

## 6. Exceptions

If ownership is unclear, the change conflicts with another controlled
source, or additional review may be required, pause publication and
escalate to the Web Operations Lead.

## 7. Records

The work-tracking item records the request and completion. Retention
follows organizational requirements.

## 8. Related documents

-   Website editorial standards
-   Accessibility guidance
-   Incident response process

## 9. Review

Review when the publishing workflow or primary content-management system
materially changes.
