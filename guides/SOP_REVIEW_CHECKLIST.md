# SOP Review Checklist

## Accuracy

-   [ ] Procedure reflects the intended current process.
-   [ ] Roles and handoffs are accurate.
-   [ ] Systems, forms, and links are current.
-   [ ] Decisions and exceptions are workable.

## Clarity

-   [ ] Title, purpose, and scope are clear.
-   [ ] Steps use direct language.
-   [ ] Terms are defined where needed.
-   [ ] Requirements and optional guidance are distinguishable.

## Control

-   [ ] Document ID and version are present.
-   [ ] Owner and approval are identified.
-   [ ] Effective date is identified.
-   [ ] Review timing follows organizational practice.
-   [ ] Related documents are identified.

## Risk check

-   [ ] No credentials, unnecessary personal data, or inappropriate
    confidential information are embedded.
-   [ ] No unsupported legal, compliance, security, or performance
    claims are made.
-   [ ] Required specialist review has been obtained where applicable.

## Release readiness

-   [ ] Placeholders are removed.
-   [ ] Superseded material will be handled.
-   [ ] Affected users will be informed.
-   [ ] Training or transition needs were considered.
