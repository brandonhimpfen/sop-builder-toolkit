# Naming and Numbering Guide

There is no universal SOP numbering system. Choose a convention that
fits your organization and use it consistently.

## Simple

`SOP-001`, `SOP-002`, `WI-001`

## Functional

`FIN-SOP-001`, `OPS-SOP-001`, `IT-WI-001`

## Principles

Identifiers should be unique, stable, searchable, free of confidential
information, and simple to maintain.

## Versioning

A possible convention is `1.0` for initial approval, `1.1` for a minor
approved revision, and `2.0` for a substantial revision. Use your
organization's established rules where they exist.

## File name example

`SOP-014_Customer-Request-Intake_v1.2.md`

Avoid using "FINAL" as a substitute for controlled version information.

## Status

Possible labels include Draft, Under Review, Active, and Retired. Define
what each means in your own process.
